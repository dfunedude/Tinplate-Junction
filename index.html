TINPLATE JUNCTION O27 tubular track · 8 × 5 ft table

Running the layout

Mouse, trackpad and touch all work the same way.

Run
Slide the throttle to apply volts. Direction cuts power and flips the train, like the old E-unit reverser. Tap a switch on the table to throw it.
Track
Tap a piece to add it at the glowing open end. Tap any ring to build from that end. Drag pieces and their ends snap together. Rotate flips the selected piece.
Train
Choose a kind of train, then pick an engine and add cars. Each kind keeps its own train, so switching back brings yours out again. Tap the engine again, or a car in your train, to repaint it. × uncouples a car. A trolley strings its overhead wire across the layout when it comes out.
View
Drag to look around. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Two-finger drag or right-drag pans. The camera button rides along behind the engine; the target button returns to the whole table. If the trains run unevenly, the gauge button switches to fast graphics: a softer picture that's quicker to draw.
Keys
↑ ↓ throttle · D direction · W whistle · F follow · G fast graphics · R rotate · Delete · Ctrl+Z undo
